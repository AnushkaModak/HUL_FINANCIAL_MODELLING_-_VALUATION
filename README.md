# ***Project Overview***
This repository contains a comprehensive Financial Model and Valuation Analysis for Hindustan Unilever Limited (NSE: HINDUNILVR | BSE: 500696). The analysis evaluates HUL’s historical financial performance (FY17–FY26), projects future operational performance, estimates intrinsic equity value using multiple valuation methodologies, and assesses risk profile and financial health.
## Key Valuation & Analysis Highlights
### 1. Valuation Models
Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) Valuation:

WACC: 11.61% (Cost of Equity: 11.57%, Pre-Tax Cost of Debt: 24.56%, Post-Tax Cost of Debt: 17.19%).

Base Case Implied Value per Share: ₹921.50 (Operating Asset Value: ₹2,13,199.4 Cr, Cash: ₹3,248 Cr, Shares: 234.9M).

### 2. Financial Metrics & Performance
Revenue & Growth: Revenue expanded from ₹33,162 Cr (FY17) to ₹64,468 Cr (FY26) with a 10-year mean sales growth of ~7.84%.

Margins Profile:

Gross Margin: ~44.7% – 48.2% (Historical Mean: 45.20%).

EBITDA Margin: ~23.3% – 24.8% (Historical Mean: 23.11%).

Net Profit Margin: ~11.7% – 23.3% (Historical Mean: 15.13%).

Return Metrics (ROIC & DuPont ROE Analysis):

ROIC: ~32.6% – 34.6% in recent fiscal years.

DuPont Analysis (FY26): ROE of 30.65% driven by Net Margin (23.33%), Asset Turnover (0.81x), and Equity Multiplier (1.63x). Asset turnover moderated post-GSK Consumer merger balance sheet expansion.

### 3. Risk Profile & Financial Health
Altman’s Z-Score: Ranging from 5.7 to 18.2 across FY20–FY26, placing HUL safely in the "Safe Zone" with negligible default risk.

Value at Risk (VaR):

Historical Approach: 95% 1-Week VaR at -9.99% (₹192.50 loss per share).

Monte Carlo Simulation: 95% 1-Week VaR at -11.17% (₹215.30 loss per share).

## Repository Structure & Financial Statements Covered
Income Statement Forecasting: Sales, EBITDA, Net Profit, and EPS projections.

Balance Sheet & Common-Size Statements: Asset, liability, and equity composition analysis (FY17–FY26).

Cash Flow Analysis: Free Cash Flow to Firm (FCFF) and Reinvestment Rate schedules.

WACC & Beta Calculations: Regression Raw Beta (0.56) and Adjusted Beta (0.67) derived from Nifty weekly returns regression.
